    Orientation

    How to read this report

    Three outcomes, four evidence classes

    REnds with the entrypoint

    Credentials tied to the compromised job or run. They end with the job or at token expiry, not necessarily when entrypoint access is revoked.

    EExpires on its own

    Credentials with a recorded lifetime, such as a role session with a time-to-live. They lapse without action, but stay usable until they do.

    ZPotentially survives

    Stored credentials that are potentially reachable from the entrypoint and stay valid after it is revoked, if the recorded path was exercised. The rotation plan is ordered around these.

    Evidence classes

    Every node and edge carries an evidence class. Severity is a label for ordering, not a verdict. In the graph, solid lines are declared or derived, dashed lines are assumed and dotted lines are unknown.

    • DECLAREDStated directly in a scanned file such as a workflow, Terraform or IAM document.
    • DERIVEDComputed from declared facts by a documented rule, for example by evaluating a trust policy as written.
    • ASSUMEDFilled in from a listed assumption where the files are silent. Assumptions are listed in the report.
    • UNKNOWNCould not be resolved. Shown separately as potential and never counted in R, E or Z.
